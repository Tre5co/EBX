How it Works — donate to the thing that happens, not the people who do it

You vote first on what the mission is, and only then on who runs it. Five phases, every week, for every cause.

    What an Earthbuck is

    One token is 10¢ of say in one mission.

    You commit your tokens to the initiative you want. They become that mission’s Earthbucks, and they finalize in steps. Until budget day, the part that isn’t final is still yours to withdraw as cash.

    Week 0 · initiative elected10% of your stake is final: deductible, and part of the pool.
    Week 8 · organization electedAnother 10% of your organization-election stake is final. Backers of a losing choice can move into another mission.
    Week 15 · budget dayEverything is final. You hold the mission’s coin: keep it, or exchange it for a different mission.

    What the network is for

    Research and news are how a mission gets decided and run, not decoration.

    Research the mission to win Rewards

    1. Background

      Researches the cause — the state of it and the news around it — and contextualizes the initiatives standing in it. Opens when the cause is confirmed

    2. Investigation

      Investigates the organization that would run the initiative — leadership, proposal, credibility — and compares it with the others. Opens at week 0

    3. Analysis

      Researches the initiative and philanthropy together, and weighs the action plan against comparable programmes. Opens at week 8

    One Background per person per cause, one Investigation per organization, one Analysis per mission. The most-voted Background and Investigation lead each mission's Analyses — 3/32 of every pool is paid out on those results. How to post →

    And we bring you the news

    Every mission you fund is covered like a story, for as long as the money is moving. You donate, we follow.

    1. Journalists

      Publish the mission’s progress live on its page — what was promised, what arrived, what it cost. They travel

    2. Scientists

      Verify the initiatives and vet the organizations, and keep the research scientifically honest. They travel

    3. Auditors

      Track every dollar sent against what the organization said it would spend, and report the difference. Public ledger

    This is what a slice of every pool buys: not a receipt, a correspondent.

    A public forum budgets the missions

    1. Service

      Labor required. Something we can send people to do — a job, an hourly rate, and the days it takes.

    2. Supply

      Commodities required. What those people need to do it — an item, and what it costs.

    3. Support

      Connections required. An approval, professional help, legal help in a conflict. No cost line: a connection is not a purchase.

    How to post

    Three kinds of post, one way to make them

    Every post is about something. A Background is about a cause; an Investigation is about an organization; a budget item is for an initiative; an Analysis is about a mission — and it is built from the others. A general post can be about any of them, or about nothing at all.

    1. T — the initiative is elected. The most-voted Background is fixed as the mission's leading Background.
    2. T+8 — the organization is elected. The most-voted Investigation of any organization in the race is fixed as its leading Investigation. Analyses open.
    3. T+15 — budget day. The winning Analysis is elected, and the research prize is paid to it and to the research it cites.
    4. After — budget items and research carry on, and any Background or Investigation can be cited again by a later mission.

    Every edit is a new version. The version an election used stays exactly as it was. You can bring an old post into a new mission; its votes start again there.
