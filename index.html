Research the mission to win Rewards

  1. Situation

    Researches the cause — the state of it and the news around it — and contextualizes the initiatives standing in it.

  2. Investigation

    Researches the initiative, and compares the philanthropies that would run it — leadership, proposal, credibility.

  3. Analysis

    Researches the initiative and philanthropy together, and weighs the action plan against comparable programmes.

A public forum budgets the missions

  1. Service

    Labor required. Something we can send people to do — a job, an hourly rate, and the days it takes.

  2. Supply

    Commodities required. What those people need to do it — an item, and what it costs.

  3. Support

    Connections required. An approval, professional help, legal help in a conflict. No cost line: a connection is not a purchase.
