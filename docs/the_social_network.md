# The Discussion

*What the community actually does on a mission: who posts, what they post, how
posts connect to each other, and what that earns. §3 below is the research
model — the **B · I · A trio** decided 2026-09-20, which replaces the flat
three-category model in [`mission_model.md`](mission_model.md) §4. The money it
spends is the **3 research 32nds** in [`money_model.md`](money_model.md) §0; the
surfaces it lands on are `cause.html` §8 and `mission.html` §7 of
[`structure.md`](structure.md).*

## 3. The research trio — B · I · A (2026-09-20)

*Decided by Jax 2026-09-20 from the brainstorm of the same date. Legend as
elsewhere: **✅ built** · **◑ partial** · **[ ] not built** ·
**⚠ PROPOSED — needs Jax**.*

**The idea in one paragraph.** Research is three separate posts, not three
scores on one post. Each opens at a different moment in the mission, because
each depends on the one before it existing. **Authors do not connect their posts
to each other — the community does**, by voting a citation. That single move is
what makes the whole thing work: credit for a background flows from strangers
deciding it is what an analysis stands on, so nobody can route credit to
themselves, and a researcher can win on a background alone without ever writing
an investigation.

### 3a. The three post types and when they open

| Type | Opens | Gate today | Cites | Ranked on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Background** | cause confirmed | the Research tab ungreys at cause confirm | — | most helpful |
| **Investigation** | **T** — initiative elected | phase tab **c** | Backgrounds | most helpful |
| **Analysis** | **T+8** — organization elected | phase tab **d** | Investigations **and** Backgrounds | most helpful |

- The order is the dependency order. A background can be written with nothing
  but the cause; an investigation needs an elected initiative to investigate; an
  analysis needs both an investigation to reason from and an organization to
  reason about.
- Nothing closes early. **Backgrounds stay open the whole mission** — a
  background posted in week 10 is allowed, and is judged on whether it brought
  something new, which its post date (3g) lets every reader check for themselves.
- Analysis runs through the framing phase and ends on budget day, so the
  strongest analyses are in front of the community while the budget is being
  set.

### 3b. One wall per type

- **One post per type, per person, per mission.** Three walls, not one. This is
  the anti-slop rule: a person who wants more presence has to write a better
  post, not more posts.
- One person **may** win in more than one category in the same mission. Being
  deeply invested in a mission is the behaviour we want.
- ✅ Already the intent of `structure.md` §5 — "the users 3 research posts
  should be displayed and editable, toggled by mission".
- ✅ **Membership gates winning, not posting** (`mission_model.md` §4). Anyone
  in scope may post; only a mission member can take a reward.

### 3c. Voting

- **No star scales.** The three-scale idea (rating every post on background,
  investigation and analysis) is dead — once a post has a type, rating it on the
  other two means nothing.
- ✅ One vote per post, the existing **helpful · neutral · harmful**, with
  neutral a real signal (`mission_model.md` §4, and the `hot` weights in
  `structure.md` §10).
- **Voting is optional**, and so is citing. A reader can vote without citing,
  cite without voting, or read and do neither.

### 3d. Citations — the community connects the posts

A **citation** is a second, separate vote: *this earlier post is what this
later post stands on.*

- **Who casts it:** any reader, on a post they did not write, naming a post they
  did not write.
- **What it names:** from an **Investigation** → one **Background**. From an
  **Analysis** → one **Investigation** and one **Background**, independently.
  The analysis reaches the background **directly**, so a citation chain can
  never split up an investigation and a background that belong together.
- **The author may suggest, not decide.** A poster can link the posts they
  actually used and say which one they think should be cited. That suggestion is
  displayed, and it is *not* a vote. The tally the community casts is what
  counts.
- **⚠ Suggested candidates are shown** next to the citation control — ranked by
  shared link rails (same initiative / same organization / same budget items),
  then by text overlap, then by existing citations. Five at a time, plus search.
  Without this, people cite whatever is already on top and the first post ever
  written wins forever.
- Citations are visible live and keep accruing until the lock (3e). They do not
  freeze when the next category opens.

### 3e. Scoring and the 3/32

Research is paid out of the **3/32** already reserved in `money_model.md` §0 —
1/32 per category. **The split inside each category is the decision of
2026-09-20:**

| Category | Pool | Paid to | ⚠ Proposed rank split |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Background** | 1/32 | top **3** | 3 : 2 : 1 (½ · ⅓ · ⅙ of the 32nd) |
| **Investigation** | 1/32 | top **2** | 2 : 1 (⅔ · ⅓ of the 32nd) |
| **Analysis** | 1/32 | top **1** | whole |

Why this shape: an analysis is the hardest and rarest post, so its 32nd is
undivided and worth the most to one person; backgrounds are the most
approachable, so that 32nd is spread widest and gives the most newcomers a
reason to start. It also settles the thing that was hard about this all along —
the three categories no longer compete with each other for the same money, so
they no longer have to be motivated the same way.

**Citations feed the ranking; they are not a separate prize.**

```
category score = own votes + (citation weight × citation votes received)
```

- A background that an analysis cites gets **elevated** by that citation — but a
  plainly better background can still out-rank it. That is the whole point of
  paying by category rank rather than by trio.
- ⚠ **Proposed weights:** a citation from an **Analysis** counts **1**; a
  citation from an **Investigation** counts **½**, because the analysis is the
  final word on what the research actually rested on.
- A citation vote naming your own post does not count. Neither does an author's
  own suggested link.
- ⚠ **Proposed lock:** all three categories lock and pay on **budget day
  (T+15)** — the date the money model already treats as final. Scores are a
  snapshot at the lock; everything is live and visible until then. *(This
  differs from the three separate pay dates in `mission_model.md` §4 — see 3k.)*

### 3f. The report trio

The progress report needs one coherent piece of research per mission, not a
leaderboard. That piece is the **trio**:

> the **winning Analysis**, plus the **Investigation** and the **Background**
> that got the most citation votes *from that analysis*.

- The trio is chosen by the community's citations, not by the analysis author.
- The trio is a **byline, not a payout** — all three authors are credited in the
  progress report as the mission's standing research. The money they get is
  whatever their category rank earned them, and the citations that put them in
  the trio are already counted there.
- A trio member may not be a category winner, and a category winner may not be
  in the trio. Both are fine: one is "the best post of its kind", the other is
  "the research this mission actually ran on".
- The trio is what gets carried into the report's research section and stays
  attached to the mission afterwards.

### 3g. Dates, edits, notifications

- ✅ **Every post shows its original post date**, permanently, next to the
  author. No "late" badge — the date is the badge, and readers judge for
  themselves.
- ✅ Posts are editable at any time, with **full edit history**.
- **New:** each entry in the edit history carries **the change in votes and
  citations since that edit** — so a post that got popular and *then* changed is
  self-evident.
- **Notified on an edit:** everyone who voted on the post, everyone who cast a
  citation touching it, the author of any post it cites, and the author of any
  post citing it.
- The review/feedback role stays: subscribing to a section of the report
  notifies you when that section changes.

### 3h. Plagiarism

The three mechanisms above do most of the work, and none of them require
Earthbux to adjudicate authorship:

1. **Post dates are public and immutable**, so a background written after an
   investigation got popular is visibly that.
2. **Edit history shows what changed and when it changed relative to the votes**,
   so absorbing someone else's work after the fact leaves a trail.
3. **The community assigns credit in the comments.** "I saw this somewhere
   first — they deserve the citation" is a comment, and the citation vote is how
   it gets acted on.

⚠ Adjudication, when it is actually needed, is the **Site Auditor**
(`roles.md` §1c) acting on a flag — the existing red-flag path, which already
floors a post's score.

### 3i. What this changes elsewhere

- **`mission_model.md` §4** — the research half of the C/I/A + B table is
  replaced by this section. Changes: **Context → Background**; **Investigation
  moves to T** (was P3a); **Analysis moves to T+8** (was P2); each 32nd now
  splits 3 / 2 / 1 ways; citations exist. Review (Case · Evaluation) and
  Budgeting (Service · Supply · Support) are **unchanged**.
- **The rename finishes here.** `context` → `situation` → **`background`**. Per
  `structure.md` §4 the backend key is still `context` and only `index.html`
  says Situation — so one rename, done in one pass, to **Background**.
- **Budgeting stays its own track.** An analysis may propose budget lines, but
  S/S/S budget posts are still upvote-only and still separate. Budget does not
  live inside the research trio.
- **`structure.md` §8** — the discussion box gains the citation control and the
  suggested-candidates rail; §7 "Leading research" gains the trio row.

### 3k. Open decisions

1. **Rank splits** — 3:2:1 and 2:1 are a proposal. Flat splits are the
   alternative, and make the 3rd background worth more.
2. **Citation weight** — 1 for an analysis citation, ½ for an investigation
   citation. This number decides how much a trio placement can out-weigh raw
   votes; it wants a look once there is real traffic.
3. **The lock date** — one lock at T+15 for all three, versus the three staggered
   pay dates already in `mission_model.md` §4 (context with the advances,
   investigation end of P3, analysis post-P3). One date is simpler to explain and
   keeps backgrounds earning citations until the end; staggered pays people
   sooner.
4. **Does an investigation→background citation pay at all**, or is it only a
   discovery aid with the analysis citation being the only one that counts?
5. **Deleting a post** — does it free the wall slot for that type, or is the slot
   spent for the mission?
6. **Sybil resistance.** Citations and votes are both money now. Self-citation is
   blocked, but alt accounts are not, and nothing above solves that.
7. **The organization's own reward** is still the missing incentive flagged in
   `mission_model.md` §4 — untouched by this change.

## 4. The four surfaces (2026-09-20)

*Where the network lives, and what the other pages do about it. Written with
the build-seq §1/§2 pass of the same date, which is the first half of this.*

**The division of labour.** The network has one home and one window:

| Surface | What it is | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| **mission.html** | **the document** | the progress report: the trio, the budget, the timeline, the org's obligations. One evolving document per mission. |
| **cause.html · News** | **the feed** | every post from every mission, one page, always the same experience. A post links to itself, with a way to open its mission. |
| **index.html · Home** | **the invitation** | what this is, and what is being written right now. Never a thread. |
| **main.html · Elect** | **the deadline** | the decision the writing is for. Never a thread. |

Home and Elect show **leading posts and counts** and link in. The moment either
one starts hosting a discussion, there are three feeds and no document.

### 4a. Home — make the clock and the writing the same diagram

1. **Each of the five panels names the post type that opens at it.** Step 1 →
   backgrounds open. Step 2 → investigations. Step 3 → analyses. Step 4 →
   budget items. Step 5 → progress reports. The wheel is currently a *money*
   clock; one line per panel makes it the **research** clock too, and the
   process stops needing a second explanation. This is the highest-value
   change on the page.
2. **Under the top card's side card, the leading post of that step's type** —
   title, author, helpful count — linking to the mission page. The wheel
   already loads everything else it would need.
3. **A "what the community is writing this week" strip**, three cells: the
   leading background, investigation and analysis across every live mission,
   each with its author and its citation count. A social network's front page
   has to show people writing. Right now the page describes three post types
   and shows none.
4. **The news band carries a live headline** instead of three links to the same
   page — the newest Earthbux News post, with its date.
5. **Signed in: your three walls.** One row showing which of your
   background / investigation / analysis you have used on the mission in focus,
   and which are still open. The one-post-per-type rule is the whole anti-slop
   design; showing the empty slots is what makes someone fill one.

### 4b. Elect — put the argument next to the ballot

1. **Every ballot row carries its research.** A tiv row reads
   *"3 backgrounds · leading: &lt;title&gt;"* and links into the mission page.
   Voting without reading is exactly the failure the trio exists to fix, and
   the ballot is the only place the two can be put side by side.
2. **Each top card gets one line of the week's most helpful post** for its own
   race.
3. **The cause toggle marks where writing is open**, the way it now marks ME
   and OE — a cause whose research rewards are still winnable is worth a mark.
4. **The post prompt fires after the vote.** "You backed &lt;initiative&gt; —
   write the investigation." Engagement is highest in the second after a
   ballot is cast, and the network's scarce resource is authors, not readers.
5. **Show the research pool as a number.** "This week's research pool: N tk —
   3 winners in background, 2 in investigation, 1 in analysis." The 3/32 is
   currently a fraction in a doc; on the Elect page it is a wage.

### 4c. What this makes the site say

Home claims *the social network for charities*. Today the page proves the
*charity* half — the pool, the elections, the runway — and asserts the
*network* half in two paragraphs of nouns. Everything above is the same
change stated five ways: **show the writing, not the categories**, and send
every click about a post to the mission page or the feed, never into a third
surface.
