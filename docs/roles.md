> Roles necessary to keep Earthbux running
**UI/UX Designer** Consistently improve and maintain the website to make it user friendly, fun, and accurate
**Site Auditor** Ensure the Content of the website, including research, posts, initiatives, suggestions, and accounts are real, appropriate, and in alignment with the rules.
**Organization Relations Manager** Verify the identity and existance of organizations vying for the prizes. Maintain communication with them and establish a mutually beneficial relationship.
**Mission Auditor** Ensure that the funds sent are being honestly reported and allocated by the organizations. Track them and record them.
**Scientist** Study the causes and initiatives rigorously and maintain scientific authenticity.
**Journalist** Recieve reporting from the scientist, relations manager, and mission auditor to construct articles for each mission.
