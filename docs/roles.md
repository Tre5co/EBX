# ROLES — the jobs Earthbux has to staff

*Every individual, from each party, the system needs a person for. One screen,
and it is the org chart: the Earthbux staff jobs, the roles a benefactor plays
inside a mission, and the roles an organization has to name before it can be
paid. Nothing here is hired yet — the point of the list is that every one of
these jobs is currently Jax's, and each line is a thing that stops happening
when it is not.*

*Where a role owns a mechanism, the mechanism is specified elsewhere:
`mission_model.md` §6 (vetting) and §7 (the agreement each organization signs),
`INSTRUCTIONS.md` `## THE PLAN` §2 (which obligations fire on which date).*

## 1. Earthbux roles

### 1a. Engineering

- **UI/UX Designer** — consistently improve and maintain the website to make it
  user friendly, fun, and accurate.
- **Venture + outreach** — develop the system, get funded, hire people, recruit
  charities, build the brand.

### 1b. Communications

- **Organization Relations Manager** — the first human contact Earthbux has with
  a counterparty, and it sets the tone for the seven weeks that follow. **Owns
  the vetting phase.**
  - Verify the identity and existence of organizations vying for the prizes.
  - Maintain communication with them and establish a mutually beneficial
    relationship.
  - Deliver the spiel to the org.
  - Help them get nonprofit status.
  - Give them the check; tell them about our resources.
  - Automatic Earthbux posts — "publish the winner", as automatic draft
    creation: one for the initiative election, one for the organization election
    (with claim link and countdown).
  - Enforce deadlines.
- **Scientist** — study the causes and initiatives rigorously and maintain
  scientific authenticity.
  - Research, vet and report on orgs.
  - Verify the initiatives.
  - Travels.
- **Journalist** — receive reporting from the scientist, the relations manager,
  and the mission auditor, and construct articles for each mission.
  - Report on orgs.
  - Publish progress live on the mission page.
  - Travels.

### 1c. Management

- **Site Auditor** — ensure the content of the website (research, posts,
  initiatives, suggestions, accounts) is real, appropriate, and in alignment
  with the rules.
  - Moderates discussions.
- **Mission Auditor** — ensure the funds sent are being honestly reported and
  allocated by the organizations; track them and record them.
  - Vetting of orgs.
- **Beneficiary relations** — testimonial to Earthbux *and* to the orgs, on the
  website; suggestions; photo/video content; verification.

## 2. Benefactor roles (mission memberships)

- Who gets to post where.
- Voting, discussing, doing research on orgs and initiatives, building
  memberships, transactions.
